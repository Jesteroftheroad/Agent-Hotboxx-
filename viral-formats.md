# Viral food-reel format library (structures to adapt — never copy)

Each format = the SKELETON. Always rebuild with HOTBOXX dishes, prices, voice.

> DEFAULT PRESENTER: the locked HOTBOXX AI avatar host (SKILL.md §4). Every format
> below is avatar-led unless noted: the avatar delivers the hook and the payoff,
> real food footage carries the proof. That's the Scalio-style engine — the owner
> uploads raw assets, the avatar does the presenting.

## 1. ASMR money shot
Structure: 0–3s extreme close-up of the most dramatic moment (gravy pour,
cheese pull, steam burst) → 3–8s slow repeat from second angle → end on plated dish.
Audio: pure ASMR (sizzle, pour) or trending lo-fi.
HOTBOXX fit: butter masala gravy pour, tandoori sizzle, biryani steam reveal.
Caption hook: "Turn your sound on for this one."

## 2. Price-shock reveal
Structure: hook text "What ₹___ gets you in Silchar" → fast cuts of each item
landing on the table → final wide shot of the full spread + total.
HOTBOXX fit: ₹999 HotBoxx Special unboxing; "₹109 biryani" challenge.
Caption hook: "POV: you thought good biryani costs ₹300."

## 3. POV / second-person hook
Structure: "POV:" text + relatable food scenario acted in quick cuts.
HOTBOXX fit: "POV: your friend said Silchar has no good rolls" → jumbo roll montage.
Works best with manager/staff cameo or hands-only shots.

## 4. Before → after transformation
Structure: raw ingredients / plain dish → quick transition → final plated/glam shot.
HOTBOXX fit: tandoor flames → charred tikka; plain naan → butter naan glistening.

## 5. The ranking / "rating" reel
Structure: trying N items, score popups, one winner.
HOTBOXX fit: "Rating every curry at HOTBOXX" — drives comments (people argue).
Engagement bait done honestly: use real opinions.

## 6. Combo unboxing
Structure: box opens on camera, each item revealed with a beat.
HOTBOXX fit: the ₹999 HotBoxx Special — built for this format.

## 7. "Things to eat in [city]" listicle
Structure: fast-cut countdown of dishes across upbeat audio.
HOTBOXX fit: "3 things to eat in Silchar this Puja" — all three from HOTBOXX.
High shareability, tourist-friendly.

## 8. Kitchen-fire spectacle
Structure: flames, tossing, sizzle — pure visual energy, minimal text.
HOTBOXX fit: tandoor/grill footage. "Smoked. Fried. Certified." is literally this.

## 9. Duet the trend (audio-led)
Structure: take a trending audio/meme format, shoot HOTBOXX footage to it.
Requires checking what's trending THIS week — never reuse a dead audio.

## 10. Offer-first promo reel
Structure: offer text in first second ("50% OFF all drinks") → supporting food shots.
HOTBOXX fit: direct-order promos. Use sparingly — max 1 in 4 posts or the page
starts looking like a flyer.

## 11. Avatar taste-test reaction
Structure: avatar hypes the dish to camera (0–3s) → hard cut to real close-up
money shot (pour/pull/steam) → avatar's over-the-top reaction → CTA.
HOTBOXX fit: new Puja-menu items. The avatar is the hype man; the food is the proof.

## 12. Avatar price-drop reveal
Structure: avatar plays on the "eating out is expensive" worry → reveals the real
HOTBOXX direct-order price over the actual dish → "WhatsApp to order" CTA.
HOTBOXX fit: ₹109 everyday biryani, ₹79 chicken roll, "save up to 40% vs
Swiggy/Zomato" messaging. Never invent competitor prices — HOTBOXX prices only.

## 13. The Scalio formula (reference — deconstructed from their demo reel)
The exact skeleton. ~12s loop, vertical 9:16.
- 0–3s AVATAR HOOK: host on camera holding the plated dish, talking direct to
  camera, pointing at the food. Energy: excited friend, not salesman.
- 3–9s MACRO FOOD B-ROLL (quick cuts, ~1–2s each): extreme close-up money shots —
  sauce dripping off the piece, salt crystals on fries, fork lifting a glazed wing,
  top-down flat lay with a hand reaching in + drink can and dip bowl beside the plate.
- 9–12s AVATAR PAYOFF: back to host with the dish, pointing, delivering the CTA
  line. Ends visually where it began → loops cleanly.
Avatar look (reference): young Indian man, early 20s, casual black t-shirt, dark
restaurant interior with warm strip lighting. Relatable local-guy energy — not a
model, not a chef.
HOTBOXX fit: minimum input = ONE dish photo. Avatar hook + payoff generate around
it; macro B-roll via image-to-video on that photo (drip, steam, sizzle) plus a
top-down restyle. Works for any hero dish: tandoori, butter masala, biryani, the
₹999 Special.
