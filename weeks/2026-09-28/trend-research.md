## What's working right now (Sep 2026)
- **Process beats plated.** Indian restaurant reels showing process (tandoor
  bread puffing, cheese/gravy pulls, sizzlers landing) get far more shares than
  static dish shots ([Restaurant India](https://www.restaurantindia.in/article/how-instagram-is-shaping-food-trends-in-india.15972), [DineCard](https://www.dinecard.in/blog/restaurant-instagram-reels-ideas-increase-footfall)).
- **Raw phone footage beats studio.** Unpolished, funny reels outperform
  studio-shot content for small Indian food businesses ([Restaurant India](https://www.restaurantindia.in/article/how-instagram-is-shaping-food-trends-in-india.15972)).
  Good for us: the manager's real shots are the proof layer.
- **Visual drama + disbelief** is one of two winning food lanes this month;
  the other is satisfying step-by-step with strong cooking sound ([Lightreel, Sep 2026](https://lightreel.ai/blogs/whats-trending-on-instagram), [MenuTiger](https://www.menutiger.com/blog/social-media-food-trends)).
- **Hidden-reveal / teaser mechanics** are trending for restaurants teasing a
  special or upcoming menu ([Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/)).
- **Festival specials** (Eid biryani, Puja menus) are a named top-performing
  category for Indian restaurants ([DineCard](https://www.dinecard.in/blog/restaurant-instagram-reels-ideas-increase-footfall)).
- Audio: pick from this week's list at post time — [Scott Social trending audio, Sep 2026](https://www.scottsocialmarketing.com/blog/trending-reels-audio-this-week-on-instagram). Clips ship silent.

## Top 3 formats for this week

### 1. Avatar taste-test × process shot (Scalio formula, formats #13 + #11)
- **Hook (0–3s):** host holds the dish to camera, points, "Silchar, you're not
  ready for this."-style energy (wording set in hooks.txt).
- **Shots:** macro process B-roll from the manager's photo — gravy pour, steam,
  naan tear (3–9s) → host reaction + CTA, loop back to opening frame (9–12s).
- **Audio:** trending upbeat track or pure ASMR sizzle.
- **Why HOTBOXX:** hits the "process beats plated" signal and needs only ONE
  dish photo. Best dish: Chicken Butter Masala — also carries the live FREE
  tandoori roti promo (direct orders).

### 2. Price-shock reveal — everyday price (formats #12 + #2)
- **Hook:** host plays on "eating out in Silchar is expensive" → reveal.
- **Shots:** host setup → real biryani steam reveal / roll cut-open → host
  payoff with WhatsApp CTA.
- **Why HOTBOXX:** ₹109 Chicken Biryani and ₹79 Chicken Roll are genuinely
  shocking prices — disbelief is this month's winning lane. Everyday items, so
  it converts now, not only during Puja. HOTBOXX prices only; no competitor
  prices.

### 3. Puja teaser — "hidden reveal" of the ₹999 HOTBOXX Special (formats #6 + reveal trend)
- **Hook:** host with a closed box: "Puja plan for the whole gang?"
- **Shots:** box opens → each item revealed on a beat (biryani, chicken kosha,
  mutton kosha, naan, coke) → full spread → host payoff.
- **Why HOTBOXX:** festival specials are a top category; box-reveal is built
  for group-order sharing ("tag your Puja squad"). Save-worthy 3 weeks out.
- **BLOCKED ON:** (a) the owner confirming Puja dates (16 vs 17 Oct), (b) which
  biryani is in the Special, (c) whether the manager can shoot the actual box/
  spread — it's a combo, so AI-invented food would violate the real-food rule.
